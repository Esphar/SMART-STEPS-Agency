# SMART STEPS Agency

Welcome to the official repository of **SMART STEPS Agency** — a digital platform dedicated to helping youth and job seekers in Rwanda and beyond access verified opportunities and gain essential skills.

## What We Do

Smart Steps provides:

- **Job Announcements** (Imirimo)
- **Scholarships**
- **Internships**
- **Application Assistance**
- **Digital Literacy Tutorials** (Amasomo)

We publish timely and reliable information to help people take smart steps in their careers, education, and personal development.
